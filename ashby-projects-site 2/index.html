01 / STAMINA
STAMINA · PLATFORM ONE

Every Saturday night, the century-old tunnels beneath Flinders Street come alive. Stamina is Ashby Projects' purpose-built home for hard techno, hard bounce, and everything living on the faster edges of the 4/4 spectrum — a club engineered for a crowd that doesn't come to stand still.

Set inside the heritage-listed Banana Alley Vaults, Stamina is built around raw arches and low ceilings, reworked into a modern rave environment. Across three distinct rooms, NEXO sound systems and more than three kilometres of neon create an atmosphere that feels found rather than built.

Programming brings Melbourne's own scene together with international artists, with no ceiling on BPMs and no interest in trends. Built by the scene, for the scene.

NERVE · BROWN ALLEY

Since launching in December 2023, Nerve has grown into one of Melbourne's most consistent Friday night institutions — a weekly gathering for the city's dance music community, built on a simple idea: the music comes first, every single week.

Housed inside Brown Alley, a multi-level nightclub on Lonsdale Street, Nerve moves between driving techno, hard house, trance and garage. Sharp programming, a dialled-in sound system and lighting that turns the room into part of the performance keep the night feeling alive.

Nerve is where the Ashby Projects story properly began. Together with Stamina, it gives Melbourne's harder music community a full weekend circuit: Fridays at Nerve, Saturdays at Stamina.
