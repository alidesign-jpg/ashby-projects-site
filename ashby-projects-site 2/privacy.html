Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated 5 November 2026

Terms & conditionsPrivacy policy

About this policy

Ashby Projects Pty Ltd (ABN 58 690 428 285) ('Ashby Projects') is committed to ensuring your privacy through compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) (Privacy Act), EU General Data Protection Regulations and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This privacy policy details how Ashby Projects collects, uses, stores and deals with personal information.

Information we collect

Ashby Projects, in the course of conducting its business collects a range of personal information including, amongst other things, names, email addresses, mailing and residential addresses, business and home phone numbers.

Ashby Projects only collects personal information that is reasonably necessary for one or more of Ashby Projects' functions or activities.

Ashby Projects only collects sensitive information if it is reasonably necessary for Ashby Projects' functions or activities or an exception applies under the Privacy Act and the individual to whom the information relates has given their express consent to the collection.

No automated decisions are made with the data collected. Collection of personal information by Ashby Projects is only done by lawful and fair means.

How we collect information

Ashby Projects collects personal information about an individual directly from that individual wherever it is reasonable and practicable to do so. This may be through contact forms, letters, emails, websites, receipts, telephone conversations, consultations and attendance at events.

Ashby Projects will always take reasonable steps to ensure individuals are aware of the fact and circumstances of collection, the purpose of collection and information about Ashby Projects' privacy policy.

How we use and disclose information

Ashby Projects will only use or disclose personal information for the Primary Purpose including things such as managing our relationships, performing administrative functions, informing customers and others about our products and services by direct marketing methods, market research, building and maintaining business relationships, implementing our communication model and complying with legal requirements.

Where Ashby Projects uses personal information other than for the Primary Purpose, Ashby Projects does so in accordance with the Privacy Act and APP 6.

Direct marketing

Ashby Projects engages in direct marketing of its products and services from time to time.

Ashby Projects only uses personal information for direct marketing in circumstances where Ashby Projects has collected the information directly from the individual and that individual reasonably expects to receive direct marketing material from Ashby Projects.

Ashby Projects offers all individuals the opportunity to unsubscribe from receiving direct marketing communications. It is not mandatory to have your personal information recorded by Ashby Projects to get access to our free content, however this helps us keep you up to date on latest initiatives and news.

Access and correction

Ashby Projects takes all reasonable steps to ensure that the personal information it collects, uses and or discloses is accurate, complete, up to date and relevant.

You have the right to ensure that your personal information which is held by Ashby Projects is accurate. To make a request to access and or correct your personal information, please contact Ashby Projects' Privacy Officer using the contact details provided below.

While Ashby Projects will always endeavour to give access to personal information free of charge, Ashby Projects reserves the right to charge individuals for access to personal information to cover costs associated with:

  • searching for, locating and retrieving personal information and deciding which information to provide to the individual;
  • using an intermediary; and
  • reproducing and sending the information.

Storage and security

Ashby Projects takes all reasonable steps to ensure that information held by Ashby Projects is safe and secure, and that it is protected from misuse, loss, unauthorised access, modification and disclosure. No data is sold or shared with other companies for marketing purposes.

Ashby Projects stores and secures personal information via third party data storage providers including our ticketing platform, eventflo. Ashby Projects will make all reasonable endeavours to ensure the storage provider is compliant with the APPs.

Retention and deletion

Ashby Projects will take reasonable steps to de-identify or destroy personal information when it is no longer needed by Ashby Projects except where the information forms part of a Commonwealth record or Ashby Projects is required by law or a court or tribunal order to retain the information.

Anonymity and pseudonyms

Individuals have the option of dealing anonymously or by pseudonym with Ashby Projects unless it is impracticable to do so or Ashby Projects is required by law or a court or tribunal order to deal with identified individuals.

Opting out and removal

Ashby Projects stores data until the user opts out or specifically requests to be removed from our database. Once removed all records of the information is removed from our database. If you wish to opt out please get in contact with our Privacy Officer.

Cookies and remarketing

Ashby Projects uses cookies through third party providers including Facebook and Google. This is so we can improve our experience of our website visitors and provide relevant re-marketing campaigns on promotions that we may be running to visitors.

To opt out of remarketing we recommend clearing browser history and cookies regularly and researching the way to opt out of being tracked through Google and Facebook permanently through their relevant sections of their websites.

Changes to this policy

Ashby Projects reserves the right to amend this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on our website.

Complaints

If you believe there has been a breach of the APPs or an APP Code, please contact Ashby Projects' Privacy Officer using the details provided at the bottom of this page.

Ashby Projects takes privacy complaints very seriously. If you make a complaint, Ashby Projects will respond within 5 working days to let you know who is responsible for managing your complaint. Ashby Projects will try to resolve your complaint within 10 working days. When this is not possible, Ashby Projects will contact you within that time to let you know how long we will take to resolve your complaint.

Ashby Projects will investigate your complaint and where necessary, consult with third parties about your complaint. Ashby Projects will make a decision about your complaint and write to you to explain our decision.

Individuals can also complain directly to the Office of the Australian Privacy Commissioner. Details about how to file a complaint can be found at www.oaic.gov.au or by calling 1300 363 992.

Contact our Privacy Officer

All queries and complaints should be directed to the Ashby Projects Privacy Officer.
